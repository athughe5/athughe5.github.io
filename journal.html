A Night in London

Wish you were here!

London, England

  • We got to our apartment around 6 PM and headed straight to The Cumberland Arms to grab a drink!

Camden + City Views

London, England

Munich, Germany

Exploring Munich

Wish you were here!

Munich, Germany

  • After an early flight from London and a quick train ride, we finally arrived at our apartment in Munich!.
  • We walked to the English Gardens to explore and enjoy some ice cream on the grass!.
  • We watched the surfers jump into the water waves, and some people jumped in to float down the stream!

Oktoberfest Festivities

Wish you were here!

Munich, Germany

  • Monday morning, we woke up and walked to Bavarian Outfitters to pick up the Drindls and Lederhosen that we rented! The store is very busy and it is better to get there earlier than later, especially considering sizes and styles run out quickly despite reservations!
  • Once we made it back to our apartment and got ready, we walked to Oktoberfest!
  • We visited several beer tents, and tried the different beers offered while listening to live music (there was a lot more English songs than expected)!
  • We tried some traditional Bavarian food such as Schweinshaxe, Wiener Würstchen, and of course Brezeln!
🇩🇪
