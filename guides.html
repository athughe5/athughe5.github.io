Routes + Recommendations!

London, England

Getting Around

  • Travelling to London, we took a British Airways direct flight from Phoenix Sky Harbor, and the total flight time was around 8 hours and 30 minutes. We took a round trip flight, and the return flight was around 10 hours and 10 minutes.
  • Once in London, we took an uber to our AirBNB, and relied on the Tube afterwards. The daily maximum depends on the zones in which you travel, but ranges from £8.90 to £16.30. While the Tube is afforable, and I recommend using it over Uber, be mindful that it closes around midnight on weekdays.
  • Much of the city, especially the more popular tourist attractions, is very walkable, and I highly recommend walking whenever possible in order to get the most out of your time there, and see as much of the culture and sights as possible!

Where to Stay

  • For your stay in London, I would recommend staying near Westminster or Kensington, and make sure your apartment or hotel is close to a Tube station!
  • We stayed in Kensington near Holland Park, and the location was great! We were only a few minutes away from the nearest Tube station and convenience store, as well as many pubs!
  • I definitely recommend booking your hotel or apartment as early as possible, as prices can get high far in advance! You can also look for accomdations that allow cancellations with full refunds if you are hesitant, that way you can still search for other options, but you have a backup plan!

Must-See Stops

  • Visit Big Ben and Westminster Abbey.
  • Walk across Tower Bridge.
  • Explore Borough Market and Camden Market.
  • See the Buckingham Palace and changing of the guard.
  • Go on the London Eye and take in the best views.
  • Explore Hyde Park and enjoy a nice picnic!
  • Visit Notting Hill and embrace the iconic Rom-Com atmosphere!

Don't Forget...

  • Comfortable walking shoes!!!!!!
  • A power adapter - regular two pronged plugs will not work!!
  • Umbrella or a light rain jacket for the drizzle.
  • A portable phone charger if you expect to be out all day.
  • I would recommend adding a card with no foreign transaction fees to your Apple Pay or equivalent touch-to-pay apps for easy access in the Tube stations!

Munich, Germany

Getting Around

  • Travelling to Munich, we took a direct Lufthansa flight from LHR to MUC. The flight was around 1 hour and 20 minutes on the way there, and closer to 2 hours and 30 minutes on the return flight to London.
  • Once in Munich, it took us some time to figure out the trains, but we ended up purchasing a group day ticket, which covers up to 5 adults, and took the S-Bahn all the way to our apartment.
  • Everything in the city was very walkable for our family, and we only had to order one uber during the trip, only to arrive at our destination faster, but otherwise walking from place to place was incredibly convenient.

Where to Stay

  • We stayed in the Old Town area in Angerviertel, and the location was incredible! We were walking distance to many shops and restaraunts and only about 20 minutes from the English Gardens.
  • It is INCREDIBLY important to book your hotel as early as possible if you plan on going to Munich for Oktoberfest. Prices rise very quickly and hotels will sell out fast!! Make sure to be watching the prices of hotels in advance and book as soon as you book your plane tickets!
  • Pay attention to the location in which you are booking! We were only about a 25 minute walk from the festival grounds, which was convenient, but it gets cold at night and when you are tired from being on your feet all day long, you will likely not want to walk any distance farther. Be mindful of this when booking!

Must-See Stops

  • GO TO OKTOBERFEST! If your trip is planned in late September/Early October, this is an absolute necessity, and one of the coolest experiences ever.
  • Explore the English Garden, and enjoy a beautiful picnic! Towards one of the ends of the park, surfers will jump into the water waves, which is super entertaining to watch!
  • Visit Marienplatz, the historic town square.

Don't Forget...

  • Comfortable walking shoes!!!!!!
  • Longer pants and long sleeve tops - it gets chilly at night!
  • Euros for vendors, especially at Oktoberfest as they will not accept card! It is important to not have all of your cash in one place, and best to split it up among group members in case of emergency!
  • A power adapter - regular two pronged plugs will not work in Munich either!!
