About me!

Hi, I'm Addi!

Welcome to Addi Abroad! I'm currently a student in college with a hugepassion for exploring new places, learning about different cultures, and creating important memories around the world. Whether it's wandering through the streets of London, relaxing on the Amalfi Coast, or hiking through Japan's bamboo forests, I love experiencing new things and travelling to new cities!

Why I Love to Travel

Traveling gives me the ability to step outside of my comfort zone, try new things, and gain new perspectives on the world. Every destination has taught me something different, and I believe some of the best lessons life has shown me has come from experiencing something foreign...literally.

Why I Created Addi Abroad

I created Addi Abroad, because I wanted a place to document the thing I am most passionate about -- travelling! I also want to be able to share my recommendations as well as memories from my experiences. I hope that this website can inspire others to put themselves out there and travel to a new place!

Quick Facts

  • Favorite destination: Capri, Italy
  • Dream destination: Oeschinen Lake in Switzerland!
  • Travel style: Carry-on...if my souvenirs fit!
  • Favorite season to travel: Summer
